Document Readers

Extracting text from a PDF requires a layout-aware parser. Most PDFs encode glyphs at absolute coordinates and the reading order is implicit.

DOCX files are ZIP archives containing XML parts. The body lives in word/document.xml. Tables, lists, and headings preserve structure.

XLSX uses the SpreadsheetML schema. Cells reference a shared string table, formulas use A1 notation, and styles are stored centrally.

Encoding

HTML, TXT, and EPUB content may arrive in UTF-8, GB18030, or Latin-1. A three-step fallback chain matches typical ingestion pipelines.
